# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being welcoming to people whose experience differs from yours
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## A note specific to this project

This repo records its architecture decisions in `docs/adr/` and treats them as settled for routine
work. Disagreeing with a decision is welcome — that is what new information is for. Relitigating one
repeatedly, or treating a maintainer's "no" as an invitation to argue harder, is not. A closed thread
is a closed thread.

## Enforcement

This project has no separate conduct address. Report unacceptable behaviour through
**[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/jpslav/keel/security/advisories/new)** — a public
repo's only private inbound channel, the same one used for security reports, and read only by the
maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
violate this code, and will explain why when they do.
